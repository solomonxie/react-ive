05 — Out-of-Band Swaps

Inbox 0 unread

Clicking the button posts to POST /api/notify. The server returns two things in one response: a new <li> for the list below (the normal hx-target swap), and an hx-swap-oob="true" fragment carrying a fresh #notif-count badge, which htmx swaps into place out of band — no matter where #notif-count lives in the page.
